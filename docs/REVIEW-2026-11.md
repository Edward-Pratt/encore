# Code review, November 2026

A full review of the plugin after reports from a user: lyrics that loaded and then
switched to "Instrumental", a Liked Songs favourite that did not play, and a seek bar
that moved with nothing playing. Every module was read. Each finding below is fixed
unless marked otherwise; the commit names the fix.

| # | Where | Symptom | Severity | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `lyrics-lookup.luau` `rank` | An "instrumental" answer ranked equal to the best possible hit, so it replaced lyrics already shown and ended the lookup. NetEase often matches an instrumental release of the same length. | High | Instrumental ranks below plain lyrics; NetEase prefers a release with lyrics and only calls a reply instrumental when the marker is all it holds; instrumental is not shown mid-lookup. `66ad996` |
| 2 | `service-lyrics.luau` `fresh` | A cached "instrumental" never expired, whatever its cache version, so a wrong one stayed for good. | High | Instrumentals expire after a week like misses; cache version 7 drops the old entries. `66ad996` |
| 3 | `widget.luau` `render` | With no player open the widget hid itself, so it vanished from the bar and the panel (which starts music apps) was out of reach. | Medium | A music icon stays that opens the panel; `hide_when_idle` still hides it. `4b44447` |
| 4 | `service-launch.luau` | Liked Songs was sent to Spotify five times, 3 s apart, waiting for a Playing status that never came: Spotify only opens that page (see DESIGN.md, "Liked Songs only opens"). | Medium | `favourites.playable`; sent once (twice after starting Spotify), an OSD says to press play; the panel and launcher show it as Open. `a23ebb9` |
| 5 | `service-players.luau` / `media.luau` | A player saying Playing whose position does not move (Spotify while loading or stuck) kept the seek bar running. | Medium | `position-sample.luau` marks it `stalled`; surfaces hold its position. `a23ebb9` |
| 6 | `lyrics-query.luau` `DASH` | " – " and " — " never split titles: the en and em dash are multi-byte, and a Lua character class matched their single bytes. Version tails and "Artist — Song" video titles went uncleaned. | Medium | One pattern per dash; the earliest wins. `204a8f3` |
| 7 | `lyrics-kugou.luau` `pick` | Kugou's fuzzy search result was picked by length alone, so another artist's song of a similar length could supply the lyrics. | Medium | Candidates must match the artist (`query.sameArtist`, shared with NetEase). | 8 | `lrc.luau` `stripCredits` | Any line with a non-ASCII label before a colon was dropped as a credit, so mid-song lyrics such as "她说：我爱你" vanished. | Medium | Only the leading and trailing credit runs go. A credit-shaped lyric right next to them still goes. `204a8f3` |

## Noted, not changed

- `service.luau` runs `hyprctl clients -j` on every `players` publish (track changes,
  seeks, volume) as well as every 10 s. A cost, not a bug.
- `service-art.luau` never retries a cover whose download failed until the plugin
  reloads.
- `service-players.luau` keeps a `CanQuit` entry per bus name forever; browsers make a
  new bus name per instance, so it grows slowly over a long session.
- A track whose player reports a stale length at a track change is looked up twice
  (the key includes the length); DESIGN.md explains why the length stays in the key.
- "Chinggis Khaan" (Batzorig Vaanchig) shows no overlay: LRCLIB and NetEase have only
  untimed lyrics for it and Kugou has none. Correct behaviour; the panel shows the plain
  lyrics.

## Verified

- `lua tests/pure.lua`: new cases for each lyrics fix, the lookup ranking, position
  samples and `favourites.playable`; each lyrics case fails on the old code.
- `noctalia plugins lint encore`: no errors or warnings.
- Spotify's `OpenUri` was tried live with every Liked Songs URI form and with a playlist
  and a track.
